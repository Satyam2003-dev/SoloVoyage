Summary

  • SoloVoyage has no accounts and no database. What you type is saved in this browser's local storage.
  • Local storage is not encrypted. Anyone who can use this browser profile, and some browser extensions, can read it.
  • The site never asks for passport numbers, document scans or payment details. Please do not type them into free text fields.
  • Clearing your browser data deletes your SoloVoyage data. Private windows usually forget it when closed.

Your data

Data controls need JavaScript. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Visit statistics

The earlier SoloVoyage site used Google Tag Manager on every page. It is now off until you allow it. If you allow it, Google receives technical information about your visit, such as pages viewed, device type and IP address, under Google's own terms, and the tags configured in the container may set cookies. SoloVoyage's code does not send your trips, lists or contacts to it. Page addresses can contain a search term or guide name.

Links and hosting

External links open the other site, which has its own privacy rules. Whoever hosts this site, such as GitHub Pages, may log technical details such as your IP address. Fonts, icons and images are served with the site itself.
