What it does

SoloVoyage brings 101 safety guides together with the tools to act on them: a trip planner, a checklist, a packing and document organizer, an Emergency Center and a personal library. There is no account and no server holding your data.

About the content

The 101 guides and the 10 categories come from the original SoloVoyage project. Version 2.0 edited them for clarity and added practical notes and warnings where a point needed context. Where a guide links to an official source, the link was checked on 26 November 2026; some government sites refuse automated checks.

SoloVoyage has no certifications, partnerships or professional endorsements. If you find an error, please report it on GitHub (opens in a new tab).

Offline use

Offline support needs a secure connection (https, or localhost during development) and one successful visit while online.

Available offline

  • All pages, all 101 guides, search and the Emergency Center guidance
  • Your trip plan, checklist, packing list, saved guides and contacts, which live in your browser

Not available offline

  • External links, government advisories and anything that changes live
  • Updates to the guides until you reconnect

Browsers may clear offline files when space is short. Keep a paper copy of anything essential.

Accessibility

SoloVoyage aims to follow WCAG 2.2 AA principles: keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, labelled forms, announced updates and reduced motion. It has not had an independent audit or testing with screen reader users. Please report anything that gets in your way.

Disclaimer

SoloVoyage gives general information to help you prepare. It is not legal, medical, financial, insurance or security advice, and it cannot guarantee your safety. Laws, entry rules, health risks and emergency numbers differ by country and change over time. Check current official sources for your destination, and use your own judgement. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
